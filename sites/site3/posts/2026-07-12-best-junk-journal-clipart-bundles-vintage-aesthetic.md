## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use these junk journal clipart bundles in commercial products?

Always check the specific license. Most bundles on Creative Fabrica include a commercial use license for up to 1,000 sales, but confirm the exact terms before selling journals or kits.

### What resolution should I look for in vintage clipart?

Aim for 300 DPI (dots per inch) minimum. Lower resolution PNGs will appear pixelated when printed on 8.5x11 or A4 paper.

### How do I blend digital clipart with real paper ephemera?

Print the clipart on matte sticker paper, cut with a small border, and adhere with a glue stick. Rub the edges with a distress tool or sandpaper to match the worn look of your vintage papers.

### Are there free junk journal clipart bundles worth downloading?

Yes, but free bundles often have lower resolution or restrictive licenses. Try Creative Fabrica's freebie section for high-quality options, or check [Scrapbook.com](https://www.scrapbook.com) for occasional free digital downloads.

### Can I edit the colors of vintage clipart in Procreate?

Yes. Use the "Hue, Saturation, Brightness" adjustment layer. For sepia clipart, reduce saturation by 30% and add a yellow filter (Color Balance: +15 on yellow) to match your journal's aged tones.
